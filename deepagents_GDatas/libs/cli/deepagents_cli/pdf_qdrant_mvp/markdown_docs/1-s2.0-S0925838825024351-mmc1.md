TextS1SynthesisofMn-MOF
Mn-MOF was synthesized based on the previously reported method. [1,2]
Typically, 0.247g manganese (II) chloride, tetrahydrate (MnCl₂·4H O) and 0.312g₂
1,3,5-benzenetricarboxylic acid were dissolved in 30 mL N, N-dimethylacetamide
(DMF) solution, respectively. After complete dissolution, the solutions were
ultrasonic for 5 min and mixed and stirred for 30 min. Then, the mixed solution was
transferred to 100mlTeflon-linedstainless-steel autoclaves, underwent hydrothermal
treatment at 140 ℃ for 12h. After the reaction, Mn-MOF was obtained by
centrifugation, washed three times with DMF and methanol, and dried in a vacuum
dryingovenat60℃.
